Evaluation Methodology & Mathematical Definitions

Chronological Splitting

For each user, interactions are sorted chronologically by timestamp. The earliest 80% form the training set, the next 10% form validation, and the final 10% are strictly held out for testing. This prevents future information leakage.

Relevance Threshold

An interaction in the test set is deemed relevant ($rel=1$) if the user's explicit rating is $\ge 3.5$ stars. This aligns with standard recommendation literature where only positive experiences count as successful hits.

Ranking Formulas

P@K: $|TopK \cap Rel| / K$
R@K: $|TopK \cap Rel| / |Rel|$
NDCG@K: $DCG@K / IDCG@K$
Hit Rate: $1$ if $|TopK \cap Rel| > 0$ else $0$.

Candidate Generation & Retrieval

Instead of scoring all 9,742 movies for every request, the platform first generates an elite candidate pool from 4 distinct channels:

  • Collaborative Neighborhood: Retrieves items co-rated by users with similar taste.
  • SVD Latent Space: Fast dot product projection in the 35-dimensional latent embedding space.
  • User Genre Affinity: Queries top-rated movies matching the user's favorite genres.
  • Popularity Prior: Ensures cold-start stability and catalog baseline quality.

Explainable AI (XAI) Signals

Every recommendation item includes an explainability signal indicating why the item was selected:

  • "Because you liked 'Inception' (5.0★)" — direct seed item attribution.
  • "Matches your strong preference for Sci-Fi" — genre affinity alignment.
  • "Strong alignment with underlying viewing tastes" — SVD latent factor proximity.
  • "Acclaimed by 200+ viewers (4.3★ avg)" — social proof and confidence indicator.

Real-Time Closed Feedback Loop

When a user submits feedback (rating a movie, bookmarking, or liking):

  • The payload is validated via Pydantic on POST /feedback.
  • The user's rating vector and genre affinity distribution are updated immediately in-memory.
  • Subsequent recommendation calls instantly incorporate the newly rated items and exclude seen items!
